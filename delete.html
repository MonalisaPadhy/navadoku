Delete my account and data

For the Android game NavaDoku, package com.monalisa.navadoku.

The fastest way is inside the game

If you still have NavaDoku installed, this takes about ten seconds and happens immediately:

  1. Open NavaDoku.
  2. On the level map, tap the settings icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Confirm with Delete for ever.

The game will tell you when it is done. There is nothing else to do and nobody to wait for.

If you have already uninstalled the game

Email padhymonalisa786@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my NavaDoku account.

Because NavaDoku accounts are anonymous, we have no name or email address on file to match you by, so we need the identifier the game generated for you. If you can still open the game, it is shown at the bottom of the settings sheet. If you cannot, tell us as much as you can - the approximate date you last played and the level you had reached - and we will do our best to find it.

We will confirm within 30 days, and in practice much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its anonymous user ID
  • Your profile: stars, levels cleared, hearts, settings
  • Every level attempt and every run recorded against you
  • Your wallet and coin balance
  • Your boosters
  • Your transaction history and any reward records
  • Your daily challenge attempts

It is deleted, not deactivated. No copy is kept for analytics.

What is not deleted

  • The game's own level data. Levels, puzzles and their solutions belong to the game, not to you, and are unaffected.
  • Encrypted backups taken before your request, which are overwritten on their normal cycle within 30 days and are never used to restore an individual account.

Can I play again afterwards?

Yes. Opening the game creates a new, empty anonymous account and you start again from level 1. Nothing from the deleted account carries over - that is what deleted means.
