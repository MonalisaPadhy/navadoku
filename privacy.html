Privacy policy

Effective 19 August 2026. Last updated 19 August 2026.

NavaDoku is a puzzle game for Android. This policy says exactly what the game collects, why, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

  • The game gives you an anonymous account automatically. You are never asked for a name, an email address or a password.
  • What we keep is how you are doing at the game - levels cleared, stars, scores, attempts, coins and boosters.
  • We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, your files, your advertising ID, or anything that identifies you as a person.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anybody.
  • You can delete all of it, from inside the game or from this page, and it is really deleted.

What the game does not collect

The app requests one Android permission, INTERNET, and nothing else. It does not ask for and cannot access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, files or SMS. It does not read your advertising ID and contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Where the data is stored

Game data is stored with Supabase (supabase.com/privacy), which acts as our data processor and hosts the database on our behalf. Data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and the server.

A copy of your progress is also kept in the game's own storage on your phone, so the game works with no connection. Uninstalling the app removes that copy.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed immediately and permanently - it is deleted, not hidden, not archived and not kept "for analytics". Routine encrypted backups taken before the deletion are overwritten on their normal cycle, within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

Two ways, and both do exactly the same thing:

  • In the game: open the map, tap the settings icon, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account and data.
  • On the web: use the form on this page.

Deletion removes your profile, your progress, your attempt and run history, your wallet, your boosters, your transaction history and your account itself. You can carry on playing afterwards; the game will start you again at level 1 with a new, empty account.

Children

NavaDoku is a number puzzle with no chat, no user-to-user messaging, no user-generated content and no social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
